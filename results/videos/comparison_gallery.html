SAC on FetchReach-v3 — same task, three reward setups

All three policies were trained with identical SAC hyperparameters for 100,000 environment steps; the only difference is the reward signal (and, for the third, whether HER relabeling was used). Clips are 3 held-out evaluation episodes each, deterministic policy, seed 0.

Watch the gripper's approach: dense and sparse+HER converge smoothly toward the red target; vanilla sparse (trained on this same seed) also reaches it here, but took roughly 3.7x more training steps to get reliable at it than the other two.
